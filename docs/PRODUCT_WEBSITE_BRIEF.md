## 1. The one-liner

**Wisprnote AI is the meeting notetaker that doesn't just remember — it acts.**

It records your meetings, writes the notes for you, remembers everything across every meeting and
every tool your team uses, and then — with your approval — *takes action* in those tools: creating the
ticket, drafting the email, closing the loop. A notetaker records. A memory layer recalls. Wisprnote
**gets things done.**

**Shortest version:** *Your meetings, turned into decisions, turned into done.*

---

## 2. The problem we solve

Every team has the same leak:

1. You have a great meeting. Decisions get made, work gets assigned.
2. Someone scribbles notes (or doesn't). The recording is never watched again.
3. A week later, half the decisions never became real work. Nobody remembers who owned what.
4. The context lives in five different tools — the meeting, the ticket, the email, the doc, the code —
   and **no single tool can see the whole picture.**

Notes apps record. Other AI tools summarize. But the gap between **"what we decided"** and
**"what actually happened"** is where teams lose time, money, and trust.

**Wisprnote closes that gap.** It captures the decision, remembers it, connects it to the work across
your tools, and helps you *execute* — so nothing falls through.

---

## 3. Who it's for

- **Founders & leaders** running back-to-back meetings who can't afford to lose a decision.
- **Product & project managers** turning discussions into tickets, follow-ups, and status.
- **Engineering & technical teams** who want meeting decisions traced all the way to shipped code.
- **Consultants, client-facing & ops teams** who live in calls and need clean records + fast follow-ups.
- **Anyone drowning in meetings** who wants the notes, the action items, and the follow-through handled.

---

## 4. Why it's different (the three pillars)

| Most meeting tools | Wisprnote AI |
|---|---|
| Record + summarize one meeting | A **living memory** across *all* your meetings and tools |
| Give you a transcript to re-read | **Answer questions** across everything, with citations |
| Tell you what to do | **Does it for you** — in your real tools, with your approval |

**The three pillars to lead the site with:**

1. **Capture everything, effortlessly.** Records both sides of any call (you *and* everyone else),
   transcribes live, and writes the summary, notes, decisions, and action items automatically.
2. **One brain for your whole org.** Your meetings, plus the tools you connect (like Jira and GitHub),
   become one searchable memory. Ask anything; get a cited answer that spans all of it.
3. **It takes action — safely.** Wisprnote can create tickets, post comments, draft follow-ups, and more
   in your connected tools. Every action waits for your approval. Nothing risky happens on its own.

---

## 5. How it works (the user journey, in plain words)

1. **Hit record** (or upload an audio file). On a video call, it captures your mic *and* the call audio.
2. **Watch it transcribe live**, with speakers separated.
3. **Get instant intelligence** the moment the meeting ends: a title, a summary, structured notes, the
   key decisions, the action items (and who owns them), and the topics discussed.
4. **Ask anything, anytime.** "What did we decide about pricing last month?" "What am I on the hook for?"
   You get a clear answer that cites the exact meetings it came from.
5. **Connect your tools** (Jira, GitHub, and more). Now your meetings and your work live in one place.
6. **Let it close the loop.** Ask it to "create a ticket for that decision" or "draft the follow-up
   email." It proposes the action; you review and approve; it's done — and recorded.
7. **Stay ahead of the gaps.** Wisprnote quietly flags when a decision never became a ticket, or when
   shipped work drifted from what was agreed — so nothing slips.

## 6. Feature catalog (the build spec for feature pages)

Grouped by the value they deliver. Each item is written as a benefit you can drop into the site.
Status tags: **✅ Live** · **🔜 Coming soon** so you don't over-promise.

### 6.1 Capture — *record any meeting, hands-free*
- **✅ Records both sides of the call.** Captures your microphone *and* your computer's audio at the
  same time, so remote calls (Zoom, Meet, Teams, etc.) are recorded in full — no bots, no add-ons.
- **✅ Live recording with pause/resume** and a running timer.
- **✅ Upload existing audio** (drag-and-drop) — WAV, MP3, M4A, and more — to transcribe past recordings.
- **✅ "Are you in a meeting?" prompt** that lets you name the session as it starts.
- **✅ Never loses long sessions** — multi-hour meetings are handled smoothly, and interrupted uploads
  can be resumed where they left off.
- *Platform note: see §8. Full call-audio capture is a Mac feature today.*

### 6.2 Transcription — *accurate, speaker-aware, any language*
- **✅ Live transcription** as you speak, appearing in real time.
- **✅ Speaker separation** — your words are labeled "You"; others are separated as distinct speakers.
- **✅ Works in any language**, and can translate the notes and summary into English.
- **✅ Resilient** — keeps transcribing through brief network drops and reconnects automatically.

### 6.3 Understand — *the notes write themselves*
The moment a meeting is processed, Wisprnote auto-generates:
- **✅ A title** for the meeting (regenerate anytime).
- **✅ An executive summary** — the meeting in a few clear paragraphs.
- **✅ Structured notes** — organized by topic, with key quotes and decisions called out.
- **✅ Decisions** — what was actually agreed.
- **✅ Action items** — the tasks, each with an owner.
- **✅ Topics & their status** — what's new, ongoing, resolved, revisited, or off-track.
- **✅ People** — who was there and who was mentioned.
- *Every generated piece can be regenerated with one click; notes and titles are editable.*

### 6.4 Recall — *ask your meetings anything*
- **✅ Chat across all your meetings.** Ask a question and get a synthesized answer that **cites the exact
  meetings** it drew from. ("What were the open action items from last week?")
- **✅ Chat with a single meeting** — ask follow-ups about one specific conversation.
- **✅ Understands intent** — it knows whether you're asking for decisions, action items, a summary, a
  person's involvement, a specific fact, or a comparison, and answers in the right shape.
- **✅ Powerful search** across every title, summary, note, and transcript.
- **✅ Conversation history** — past chats are saved and easy to revisit.

### 6.5 The Knowledge Graph — *see how everything connects*
- **✅ A visual map** of your meetings, people, decisions, topics, and action items, with the connections
  between them — zoom, pan, click a node to explore.
- **✅ Connections across meetings** — see how a decision in one meeting relates to a topic in another.
- **✅ Filter and search** the map by type (meetings, people, decisions, topics).

### 6.6 People — *a directory that builds itself*
- **✅ Auto-built contact list** of everyone across your meetings, with how many meetings each attended.
- **✅ Per-person view** — every meeting a person was part of, in one place.
- **✅ Editable details** (like email) that make follow-up actions smarter.

### 6.7 Create — *turn a meeting into a deliverable*
From any meeting, generate polished, ready-to-use outputs:
- **✅ Follow-up email** — a structured draft with the recap, decisions, and assigned tasks.
- **✅ Wiki / knowledge-base document** — a clean write-up in a structured format.
- **✅ Presentation (PowerPoint)** — choose the number of slides; get a deck from the meeting.
- **✅ Formal report (Word document)** — an executive-ready report.
- **✅ Podcast script** — a two-host conversational version of the meeting.
- **✅ Concept visuals** — an AI-generated diagram of the meeting's ideas.
- *All outputs are downloadable and saved to a per-meeting library.*

### 6.8 Connect — *bring your tools into the brain*
Connect the tools your team already uses; their data joins your meeting memory so answers and actions
span everything.
- **✅ Jira** — pull in issues and comments; create and update tickets from decisions (with approval).
- **✅ GitHub** — bring in pull requests, issues, and code activity; tie shipped work back to decisions.
- **✅ Your local coding sessions** (Claude Code, Codex) — your recent engineering work becomes context
  the brain can answer about. *(Stays on your machine.)*
- **🔜 Slack, Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive** — search across them and act in them (coming soon).
- **🔜 Add any tool yourself** — connect a custom integration by URL; it plugs into the same system with
  the same controls. *(Built; rolling out.)*

### 6.9 Act — *the agent that gets things done (with your approval)*
- **✅ Agent mode in chat.** Ask it to do something multi-step ("create a ticket for today's design
  decision and assign it to Alia"). It **plans the steps, shows its work, and proposes the actions.**
- **✅ A visible plan & thought process.** You watch it work — its plan checklist, each tool it uses,
  and the result — all laid out and expandable.
- **✅ Approval cards.** Before anything changes in your tools, a card shows you exactly what it wants to
  do. You can **edit the details, approve, or decline.** (E.g., tweak a ticket's title before it's created.)
- **✅ Live for Jira today** — create issues, add comments, update status, all human-approved, with a
  link to the result and the ability to undo.
- **✅ Choose your AI** — pick the model that writes your answers (more models coming to the picker).

### 6.10 Trust & control — *you're always in charge*
- **✅ Per-tool permissions.** For every connected tool, set each action to **Allow** (runs instantly),
  **Needs approval** (you confirm first), or **Never** (blocked entirely).
- **✅ Safe by default.** Reading information is allowed; making changes asks first; deleting is blocked
  unless you say otherwise.
- **✅ Nothing risky happens on its own.** The agent literally cannot take a blocked action, and any
  action that changes data pauses for your review.
- **✅ Auditable & reversible** — actions are recorded, and writes can be undone.

### 6.11 Stay ahead — *proactive insight (the "living brain")*
- **✅ Gap alerts.** Wisprnote flags things like *"You decided to do X, but no ticket was ever created"*
  or *"the work that shipped doesn't match what was decided."*
- **✅ Decision-to-done lineage.** A visual chain from a meeting decision → the ticket → the shipped code,
  color-coded by whether the work matches the decision (on track, partial, or drifted).
- **✅ Activity pulse.** A timeline of what's moving across your connected tools and meetings.

### 6.12 Organize & collaborate
- **✅ Workspaces** — separate spaces for different teams, clients, or areas; each with its own brain.
- **✅ Folders / projects** within a workspace, with custom icons and colors.
- **✅ Move meetings** between workspaces and folders easily.
- **✅ Share a meeting** via a private link (read-only), with optional expiry, and invite collaborators.
- **✅ Manual notes** you can add and format on any meeting.

## 8. Platform & availability (be accurate on the site)

- **Wisprnote AI is a desktop app for macOS today.** The full experience — including capturing both your
  mic and the call's audio — is built for Mac.
- **Windows & Linux are on the roadmap**, not yet available. (For the site: lead with "Mac," and offer a
  "Get notified for Windows" capture if you want to collect demand.)

  account.
- **Sign-in options:** email, **Google**, or **Microsoft**.

> "Windows coming soon" note. Don't show a Windows download button yet.

## 13. The bigger story (for the About/Vision page)

A notetaker **records**. A memory layer **recalls**. An operating system **acts**.

Wisprnote already records (capture + transcription) and recalls (a searchable brain across your meetings
and tools). The leap — the reason it exists — is **acting**: turning the decisions made in a room into
real changes in the tools your team runs on, safely and with a human in the loop.

The workflow Wisprnote owns: **decide in a meeting → make it happen everywhere.**

That's the north star: not another dashboard that shows you what's wrong, but a coordination layer that
helps you *do something about it* — keeping your decisions, your tools, and your team in sync.

## 14. Glossary (use these words consistently on the site)

- **Meeting** — a recorded or uploaded session, with its transcript, notes, and intelligence.
- **The brain** — your unified, searchable memory across meetings and connected tools. *(User-friendly
  name for the knowledge layer — prefer "your brain" / "company brain" over technical terms.)*
- **Workspace** — a top-level space (team, client, area) with its own meetings and brain.
- **Folder / project** — a grouping inside a workspace.
- **Connection / integration** — an external tool you link (Jira, GitHub, …).
- **Agent mode** — the chat mode where Wisprnote plans and takes actions, not just answers.
- **Approval card** — the in-chat prompt where you review and approve an action before it runs.
- **Action / "act"** — something Wisprnote does *in* a connected tool (create a ticket, post a comment).
- **Permissions** — your per-tool rules: Allow / Needs approval / Never.
- **Gap alert** — a proactive flag (e.g., a decision that never became work).
- **Lineage** — the traced chain from a decision to the work that delivered it.
