The AGPL is not a non-commercial licence. It does not restrict *what* you use the

## What the AGPL requires in return

If you distribute Wisprnote, or a modified version, or **let anyone else interact with
it over a network**, then you must:

- Release the **complete corresponding source code** of your version, including all your
  modifications, under **AGPL-3.0** as well.
- Do so for **every user who interacts with it remotely** — this is AGPL section 13, and
  it is the clause that separates the AGPL from the GPL. Running a modified Wisprnote as
  a hosted service for other people triggers it.
- Preserve copyright and licence notices, and state what you changed.
- Not impose further restrictions on downstream recipients.

Purely internal use with no modifications distributed and no external network users is
the simplest case: run it, and you owe nothing further.
